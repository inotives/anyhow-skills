## Learning interpretation

The PDF is a vocabulary and character coverage reference, rather than a lesson plan. It gives a progression from foundational family, body, classroom, and daily-life words in Primary 1 to broader community, environment, health, safety, responsibility, culture, and problem-solving vocabulary by Primary 3.

Useful recurring domains include:

- Family and relationships
- Numbers, position, direction, size, and time
- School, classroom, learning, and play
- Food, cooking, shopping, and household routines
- Body, health, safety, and emotions
- Nature, animals, weather, and the environment
- Transport, buildings, community places, and public services
- Culture, Singapore identity, festivals, and national symbols
- Work, responsibility, cooperation, persistence, and problem-solving

## Follow-up

- Use this memo as the vocabulary coverage checklist for Singapore Primary 1–3 Chinese learning.
- If a teaching plan is needed, turn each semester into units with reading recognition, handwriting, pronunciation, example words, and sentence practice.
- The PDF is the authoritative source for the exact character grouping and should be consulted when a character-level discrepancy matters.
